Independent validation in GSE136831
Cohort in metadata: 32 IPF, 28 Control, and 18 COPD subjects; COPD was excluded.
Primary test: paired donor-level pseudobulk contrast between Club/Goblet secretory cells
and ATII within IPF; >=5 cells per state were required, yielding 15 paired donors.
Secondary LR test: broad Myeloid sender and Epithelial receiver; >=20 cells per compartment;
two-sided Mann-Whitney tests across subjects and BH adjustment over eight prespecified pairs.
These results independently reproduce a cell-state association and expression-supported
communication signal; they do not establish lineage or causal signaling.
